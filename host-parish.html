OUR HOST PARISH: SAINT ANDREW, RIVERSIDE

The 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim, is hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church of Riverside, California, with the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan SABA.

OUR STORY

Saint Andrew, Riverside

Saint Andrew began in 1989 with a handful of Orthodox families meeting in a rented room in Riverside. St. Luke parish took us under its wing and supported us for our first ten years. In 1992 we were received as a mission and named for Saint Andrew, and in 1997 we became a full parish.

Father Josiah came to us in 1998, and a year later we bought land on Canyon Crest Drive. We worshiped in the fellowship hall for nine years while we built our temple, modeled on the Church of Saint Catherine in Thessaloniki. We served the first Divine Liturgy there on Palm Sunday 2011, and it was consecrated that December.

Today we are converts and cradle Orthodox together, worshiping in English, and still building.

OUR CLERGY

The Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham has been our pastor since 1998. He was ordained in 1993, earned his Ph.D. in theology at the University of Durham, and founded Patristic Nectar Publications, which shares the teaching of the Church Fathers through audio, books and film. His Sunday homilies are on the podcast The Arena. He and his wife, Catherine, have ten children.

He serves alongside Father Thomas Hernandez, Father Jason Covarrubias and Protodeacon Elias Khoury, with Father Joseph Corrigan, retired.

Visit saintandrew.net for service times and parish life.
