Our Host Parish: Saint Andrew, Riverside

The 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim, is hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church of Riverside, California, with the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan SABA.

From a rented room to a five-acre campus

Saint Andrew began in the late 1980s, when a handful of Orthodox Christians in Riverside started gathering the names of the faithful living nearby. Services began in a rented room in 1989. The community was received as a mission and named for Saint Andrew in 1992, and became a full parish in 1997.

The Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham has served as pastor since 1998. The parish's Byzantine-style temple, modeled on the Church of Saint Catherine in Thessaloniki, held its first Divine Liturgy on Palm Sunday 2011 and was consecrated on December 3, 2011 by Archbishop Joseph of Los Angeles.

Today Saint Andrew is home to more than 250 families. Services are in English, and the community draws both converts and Orthodox families with roots in more than fifteen countries.

Visit saintandrew.net for service times and parish life.

Before You Book

The parish is in Riverside. The Convention is in Anaheim.

Saint Andrew is in Riverside, about 35 miles inland. Every Convention event takes place at the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, Anaheim, CA 92802 — an hour or more each way in traffic from the parish. Book your room in Anaheim.
