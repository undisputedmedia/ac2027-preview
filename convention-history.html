THE CONVENTION THROUGH THE YEARS

WHY ANAHEIM, WHY NOW

Every two years the Antiochian Archdiocese meets in a different city. In 2027 the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention comes to Anaheim, the first in California since Palm Desert in 2009.

Updated November 2026

WHY IT MATTERS

The Archdiocese, together in one place

The Convention is where the Archdiocese worships, decides and celebrates as one body: the General Assembly, the department meetings, the youth finals and the Grand Banquet, all in one week.

Host cities rotate across the continent, so each Convention belongs to a different region. 2027 is the West Coast’s turn, hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church of Riverside, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim.

HOST CITIES

The road to Anaheim

Each Convention has its own host parish and its own character. Here is the road to Anaheim.

1. 2005: Dearborn, Michigan

The Archdiocese met in Dearborn, home to one of the largest Arab-American communities in the country.

2. 2007: Montreal, Quebec

The 48th Convention met in Montreal, July 23–29, one of the Conventions held in Canada.

3. 2009: Palm Desert, California

The last Convention in California met at the Desert Springs JW Marriott, hosted by St. Michael Orthodox Church of Van Nuys. Until 2027, it is the most recent time the Convention came to the state.

4. 2011: Chicago, Illinois

The 50th Convention, a milestone, met at the Chicago Marriott Downtown, July 24–31.

5. 2013: Houston, Texas

Hosted by St. George in Houston, July 21–28.

6. 2015: Boston, Massachusetts

The 52nd Convention met at the Sheraton Boston, July 20–26, and Patriarch JOHN X of Antioch traveled from Damascus to preside.

7. 2017: Miami, Florida

The 53rd Convention was hosted by St. George Cathedral of Coral Gables, July 23–30. The National Bible Bowl and the Oratorical Festival finals were part of the week.

8. 2019: Grand Rapids, Michigan

The Archdiocese met in Grand Rapids, July 21–28: the last in-person Convention for four years.

9. 2021: Online

Because of the COVID-19 pandemic, the Archdiocese met virtually, July 23–24.

10. 2023: Phoenix, Arizona

The 56th Convention met at the JW Marriott Desert Ridge, July 23–30, the first in-person Convention since 2019. Patriarch JOHN X attended.

11. 2025: Chicago area, Illinois

The 57th Convention was hosted by St. George of Cicero at the Hyatt Regency O’Hare in Rosemont.

12. 2027: Anaheim, California

The 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim, hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, Riverside. Book your room and get notified when registration opens.
