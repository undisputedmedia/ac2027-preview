Where and when

When is the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention?

The 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention runs Monday, July 19, through Sunday, July 25, 2027, at the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, Anaheim, California. It is hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, Riverside, California. The Convention room block at the Hilton runs July 17–25, so you can arrive two nights early at the Convention rate.

Where is the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention being held?

At the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, Anaheim, CA 92802, beside the Anaheim Convention Center. Every service, session, exhibit and the Grand Banquet happens inside the hotel, so nothing during the July 19–25, 2027 Convention is off-site. The host parish, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, is in Riverside, but the Convention itself is in Anaheim.

Is the Convention in Riverside or Anaheim?

Anaheim. The 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention is at the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, from July 19–25, 2027. Riverside is home to the host parish, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, and is about 35 miles away by road. That is an hour or more each way in traffic. Book your room in Anaheim, not Riverside.

How often is the Antiochian Archdiocese Convention held?

Every two years, in odd-numbered years, in a different host city each time. The 2025 Convention was in Chicago. The 2027 Convention is the 58th Biennial, held July 19–25, 2027, at the Hilton Anaheim in Anaheim, California, and hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church of Riverside.

Registering

How do I register for the Antiochian Archdiocese Convention in Southern California?

Registration for the 2027 Convention (July 19–25, Hilton Anaheim) opens later in 2026, and the registration link will be posted on this site first. Tap Get Notified and we'll email you the moment registration opens. Booking a hotel room doesn't register you for the Convention.

Where can I find tickets for the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention?

There are no separate tickets. You attend the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention (July 19–25, Hilton Anaheim) by registering, which opens later in 2026. Some events, such as the Grand Banquet, are normally added on when you register rather than included in the base. Nothing is on sale yet, so be careful with anyone selling Convention tickets.

Are there early-bird registration deadlines?

Not yet announced. Registration tiers and deadlines for the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim, will be published when registration opens later in 2026. At that point they'll be posted on this site and emailed to everyone who has tapped Get Notified, on the same day.

How can I register my church group?

Group and parish registration for the 2027 Convention (July 19–25, Hilton Anaheim) opens with general registration later in 2026. Parishes send delegates to the General Assembly, so your parish office is the right first stop. Group organizers can email info@ac2027socal.com for the group instructions as soon as they are released. Hotel rooms for a group can be booked now in one transaction.

Are there group discounts?

None have been announced for the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim. Any group or parish rate will be published on this site and emailed to the list when registration opens. For hotel rooms, a parish group can book several rooms at the $209 Convention rate in one transaction using the Rooms field on the booking page.

Are meal plans available?

The meal structure for the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention (July 19–25, Hilton Anaheim) hasn't been published yet. The Grand Banquet and certain receptions are normally ticketed separately when you register. Details will be posted on this site and emailed to the list when registration opens.

Do I have to be Antiochian or a delegate to attend?

No. The 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim, is open to everyone. Parishes send delegates to the General Assembly, but you don't need to be a delegate to attend the week, and many attendees come from other Orthodox jurisdictions or are simply curious. Registration opens later in 2026. Tap Get Notified to hear the day it does.

Hotel and getting there

How do I book a hotel for the Convention?

Book inside the Convention room block at the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, through the official booking link: book.passkey.com/go/2027SoCalAntiochianArchdiocese. The Convention rate is $209 per night, held July 17–25, 2027, so you can arrive two nights before the Convention starts on July 19. Prepayment is optional.

What accommodation options are near the Convention?

The Hilton Anaheim is the Convention hotel and holds the only Convention room block for July 19–25, 2027. Every service, session and the Grand Banquet takes place inside it, so staying in the block means walking downstairs instead of driving in each morning. Hotels near the host parish in Riverside are about 35 miles from the venue.

Does booking a hotel room register me for the Convention?

No. A room at the Hilton Anaheim is a reservation with the hotel only. Registration for the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, is separate. It opens later in 2026 and will be announced on this site. Booking early holds your room, and registering later holds your place at the Convention.

What transportation options are there to the Convention?

The closest airport to the Hilton Anaheim is John Wayne / Orange County (SNA), about 12 miles away. Long Beach (LGB) is about 14 miles, and Los Angeles (LAX) and Ontario (ONT) are each about 34. Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner stops at Anaheim station, 3.1 miles from the hotel. By car, the hotel is just off Interstate 5. The Convention runs July 19–25, 2027.

Where do I park at the Convention?

Parking is on-site at the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, where every event of the July 19–25, 2027 Convention takes place. Daily rates are set by the hotel and will be confirmed closer to the date. Check the current rate with the hotel when you book. If you stay in the Convention block, you won't need to drive in each day.

The week itself

Where is the Convention schedule?

On this site's schedule page, published day by day as the committee confirms each one. The 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention runs Monday, July 19, through Sunday, July 25, at the Hilton Anaheim. Monday is arrival, the General Assembly is on Thursday, and the week closes with the Hierarchical Divine Liturgy on Sunday.

What are the main topics and events at the Convention?

The 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim, is built around daily Divine Services with the hierarchs and clergy of the Archdiocese. Alongside them run workshops, department meetings, the General Assembly, Women's Day, the Bible Bowl, the Oratorical Festival, youth programs and the Grand Banquet. The full program is still being confirmed.

Are there special events or workshops?

Yes. Workshops run in sessions throughout the week of the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim, alongside department meetings and choir rehearsals. The week also includes the Bible Bowl, the Oratorical Festival, Women's Day and the Grand Banquet. Session titles and presenters will be posted on the schedule page as they are confirmed.

Are children welcome?

Yes. The 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim, is a family event. Teen SOYO and Kidz Club (ages 4–12) run all week alongside the adult program, in their own space inside the hotel. Details and times will be posted on the schedule page as the program is confirmed.

What should I wear?

For Divine Services at the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention (July 19–25, Hilton Anaheim), wear what you would wear to church: modest, respectful clothing. Anaheim in late July is hot outside and air-conditioned inside, so a light layer helps. A dress code for the Grand Banquet hasn't been published yet and will be posted here when it is.

Is there a mobile app?

The Archdiocese runs the Antiochian Events mobile app, which has covered earlier Conventions with schedule and registration features. It hasn't been announced whether the 2027 Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim, will appear in it. Until it does, this site and the Get Notified email are the official sources for schedule and registration news.
