HOTEL & ROOM BLOCK

The Convention room block is at the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, Anaheim, CA 92802, for the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027.

OPEN NOW

Stay where the Convention happens

Every service, session and the Grand Banquet is inside this hotel. A room in the block is the difference between walking downstairs to Divine Liturgy and driving in each morning.

Divine Services, workshops, youth programs, the Bible Bowl, the Oratorical Festival, the vendor exhibition and the Grand Banquet all take place at the Hilton Anaheim. Nothing during the week is off-site.

The block runs July 17–25, so you can arrive two nights before the Convention opens on Monday, July 19, at the same rate. Booking inside the block is what holds the rate.

This is the hotel only. Event registration opens later this year.

BEFORE YOU BOOK

Stay close to the Convention

The host parish, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, is in Riverside. The Convention is at the Hilton Anaheim, about 35 miles away by road — an hour or more each way in traffic. Hotels near the parish are not near the Convention.

Does booking a room register me for the Convention?

No. A room at the Hilton Anaheim is a reservation with the hotel only. Registration for the Convention is separate and opens later this year. How registration works.

How do I get to the hotel?

The closest airport is John Wayne / Orange County (SNA). Airports, driving and parking are in the FAQ.
