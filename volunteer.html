THE TEAMS

Fourteen ways to serve

Pick one or more on the sign-up form. Here is what each team does.

Registration

Our biggest need. Staff check-in desk shifts (10:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily) and put together registration packets.

Event Support

Seat the Grand Banquet, check tickets and badges at event doors, and greet guests around the hotel.

Security

Welcome attendees, check credentials and help crowds move safely. Ages 18+, at least two shifts; training provided.

Youth/Children

Help with Child Care, teen events, the Bible Bowl and the Oratorical Festival. Department of Youth safety rules apply.

Liturgical Services

Set up the chapel, usher at daily services, and hand out bulletins and readings.

Hotel Facilities/Events

Check each event room’s setup and AV before it starts, and work with the hotel during events.

Parish Events

Help with Convention events at Saint Andrew in Riverside: setup, luncheons, parish tours and greeting guests.

Bishop’s/VIP

Drive airport transfers for the hierarchs and escort them to events during the week.

Department/Organization Liaison

Post signage, take attendance at department meetings and help assemble the General Assembly report booklet.

Logistics/Operations

Move equipment, reset rooms between events, run errands and fill gaps wherever a team needs help.

Vendors

Help vendors check in, set up and pack down, and keep the exhibit area running smoothly.

Fund-Raising

Gather Legacy Journal greetings and ads, and reach out to people who may sponsor an event.

Communications/Marketing

Help with website updates, parish mailings and photographing Convention events.

Finance

Help the Treasurer record payments, track receipts and count cash during the week, always in pairs.
