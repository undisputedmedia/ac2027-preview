WE NEED YOUR HELP

Serve at the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim. One short form covers every team.

THE TEAMS

Fourteen ways to serve

Pick one or more on the sign-up form. Registration, Event Support, Security and Youth/Children need the most help.

REGISTRATION

Check-in desk shifts and registration packets.

EVENT SUPPORT

Banquet seating, ticket checks and greeting guests.

SECURITY

Entry points and crowd flow. 18+, two shifts, training provided.

YOUTH/CHILDREN

Child Care, teen events, Bible Bowl and Oratorical Festival.

LITURGICAL SERVICES

Chapel setup, ushering and daily bulletins.

HOTEL FACILITIES/EVENTS

Room setups and AV checks before each event.

PARISH EVENTS

Events at Saint Andrew: setup, luncheons and tours.

BISHOP’S/VIP

Airport transfers and escorts for the hierarchs.

DEPARTMENT LIAISON

Signage, attendance and the General Assembly booklet.

LOGISTICS/OPERATIONS

Equipment moves, room resets and errands.

VENDORS

Vendor check-in, booth setup and the exhibit area.

FUND-RAISING

Legacy Journal greetings, ads and event sponsors.

COMMUNICATIONS/MARKETING

Website, parish mailings and event photos.

FINANCE

Payments, receipts and cash counts, always in pairs.
