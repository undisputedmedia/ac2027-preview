VOLUNTEER

WE NEED YOUR HELP

Thank you for your interest in volunteering for the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim. Your support is vital as Saint Andrew prepares to host our hierarchs and the Archdiocese.

HOW IT WORKS

Pick a team. A lead will follow up.

Every team signs up through one Saint Andrew form. Choose the teams that interest you, and a team lead will reach out with more information and next steps.

Co-chairs: Sbdcn. Christopher & Kh. Lauren

THE TEAMS

Fourteen ways to serve

Each team has a lead who coordinates its volunteers. Teams marked “Open” are still looking for a lead.

Fund-Raising Team

Lead: Open

· Journal: solicit greetings and advertisements from parishioners, businesses and organizations

· Journal: proofread content and help with layout, formatting and publishing preparation

· Journal: forward paper forms to the Registration committee for database entry

· Journal: hand out the printed journal at registration check-in (one per family)

· Sponsorship: contact prospective donors to underwrite specific events (Board Luncheon, Clergy and Clergy Wives Breakfast, youth events, gift baskets, Hierarchs’ meals)

· Sponsorship: track pledges and confirmations and report to the Fund-Raising Chair

Registration Team

Lead: Adrianna Kazanjian

Largest volunteer need: check-in runs 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily in shifts, closed during liturgical services, with extra staff at peak times.

· Work check-in desk shifts: hand out packets to pre-registered attendees and process new registrants

· Assemble registration bags and packets before the Convention (schedule, receipt, journal, badge, gift items)

· Solicit gift items from local businesses for the registration bag

· Verify full payment before releasing materials and collect the mandatory Archdiocese Registration Fee

· Check that Parent Consent and Teen Code of Conduct forms are on file before issuing youth badges

· Provide computer and printer support for the registration network (3–5 PCs, 2 printers)

Parish Events Team

Lead: Open

Supports Convention events held at Saint Andrew itself rather than the Hilton, including the Hierarchal Divine Liturgy, luncheons and parish tours.

· Prepare the church and grounds before parish events: cleaning, setup, seating, signage and parking direction for visiting guests

· Assist with luncheon service: food setup, serving, beverage stations and cleanup

· Serve as tour guides and greeters, welcoming Convention attendees and sharing the parish’s story

· Support the Hierarchal Liturgy alongside the Religious Services team: ushering, overflow seating and hospitality for visiting clergy and hierarchs

· Coordinate with the Operations team on transportation timing so parish events line up with shuttle arrivals from the hotel

Finance Team

Lead: David Romano

Supports the Treasurer in keeping Convention finances accurate, current and audit-ready.

· Help record and reconcile deposits, payments and refunds in the Antiochian Events System

· Track invoices and receipts, and prepare bills for Executive Committee approval before payment

· Help count and verify cash receipts during Convention week (always two people present)

· Help assemble supporting documents for the budget submission to the DCCP and the final financial report

· Flag discrepancies to the Treasurer immediately; never resolve them independently

Logistics/Operations

Lead: Phanourios Griffin

Supports the Operations Lead in the behind-the-scenes work that keeps every committee running.

· Help move, stage and store equipment, supplies and materials before and during the Convention

· Assist with room transitions between events: signage, furniture adjustments and resets on the hotel’s schedule

· Run errands and deliveries between committees, the hotel and off-site locations (supply runs, printing pickups, airport runs)

Sign up for this team →

Security

Lead: Shawn Kehoe

Supports the safety and good order of the Convention so that everyone can worship and gather in peace, serving with Christian love and a spirit of hospitality first. Experience in public safety, security or the military is welcome but not required; training is provided.

· Welcome and assist attendees at entry points

· Verify credentials and manage access to designated areas

· Help with crowd flow and lines at large gatherings

· Observe and report concerns through the team’s chain of command

· Support hotel staff, event staff and emergency responders as directed

· Requirements: 18 or older, available for at least 2 shifts between July 17–25, 2027, and one pre-convention training session
