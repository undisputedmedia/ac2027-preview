Visiting Disneyland Resort

The Hilton Anaheim is walking distance from the Disneyland Resort — close enough that some families build a day or two around the Convention week. This page is planning information only.

Not affiliated with the Convention. Disneyland Resort is independently owned and operated. The Convention, the Archdiocese and Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church have no partnership, sponsorship or discount arrangement with it, and no role in park tickets, hours or operations. Buy tickets directly from Disneyland Resort and confirm hours before you go.

The Resort

Two parks, one gate area

Disneyland Resort is made up of two separate theme parks and a shopping and dining district between them.

Disneyland Park

The original park, opened in 1955. Classic attractions, parades and fireworks over the castle.

Disney California Adventure

The newer, second park next door, built around California themes, with its own headline attractions and a nighttime show over the water.

Distance from hotel
Walking distance — both parks sit directly across from the Hilton Anaheim and the Convention Center.
Tickets
Sold directly by Disneyland Resort, by the day and by the park. Prices vary by date and change without notice — check disneyland.com for current pricing before you plan around it.
Hours
Published by Disneyland Resort and can vary day to day, even within the same week. Confirm the specific dates you plan to go.
Downtown Disney
The shopping and dining promenade between the hotel and the parks. No park ticket is required to walk through it.

Planning

A few things to know

Book Ahead

Tickets sell out

Popular dates can sell out, and many attractions use the Disneyland app for virtual queues. Buy tickets and make a plan before you arrive.

Time

It's a full day

Seeing either park properly takes most of a day. With services and sessions running all week, a Disney day works best on an arrival or departure day.

No Group Rate

Book on your own

The Convention has not arranged a group discount or block of tickets. Each family or parish group buys directly from Disneyland Resort.

Next Steps

Rooms are open. Registration follows.

The Convention room block at the Hilton Anaheim is open now at $209 a night. Event registration opens later this year — join the list and you'll hear the day it does.
