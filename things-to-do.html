Things to Do Nearby

The Hilton Anaheim sits in the middle of Orange County's Resort District. Every service and session happens inside the hotel, but the schedule leaves room to step out — here's what's worth it between sessions or on either end of the week. The Disneyland Resort is across the street and has its own page.

Shopping & Dining

Downtown Disney District

An open-air promenade of restaurants, shops and live music between the hotel and the parks — no park ticket required to walk through.

Food Hall

Anaheim Packing District

A restored 1919 citrus-packing building turned food hall, a few minutes' drive from the hotel — a couple dozen vendors under one roof.

Shopping & Entertainment

Anaheim GardenWalk

An outdoor retail and dining strip a short walk from the Convention Center, with a movie theater and a bowling alley.

Theme Park

Knott's Berry Farm

An older, smaller theme park about a twenty-minute drive north in Buena Park — roller coasters and an Old West-themed ghost town, run independently of Disney.

Live Sports

Angel Stadium

Home of the Los Angeles Angels, a short drive from the hotel. Check the team's schedule directly — the Convention has no role in tickets or timing.

Beaches

Huntington, Newport & Laguna

Orange County's coastline is roughly thirty to forty-five minutes west by car. Huntington Beach is the widest and most casual; Laguna is the most scenic.

History & Pilgrimage

Mission San Juan Capistrano

California's seventh mission, founded in 1776, about forty minutes south. A quiet, historic stop for anyone drawn to the older Christian landmarks of the region.

Family

Discovery Cube Orange County

A hands-on science museum in Santa Ana, a short drive from the hotel — a reliable option for younger children on a lighter-schedule day.

Theme Park

Disneyland Resort

Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure sit across the street from the hotel. Planning details are on the Disneyland page.

Good to know: everything on this page is optional and self-guided — none of it is part of the official Convention program, and the Convention has not arranged group rates, tickets or transportation for any of it. Hours, prices and availability change; check each place directly before you go.
