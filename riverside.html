A DAY IN RIVERSIDE

THE HOST PARISH’S HOME CITY

Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church is hosting the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention. Here is how to visit the parish, and seven landmarks of Riverside worth the trip.

Updated November 2026

PLAN THE TRIP

Plan it as a day trip

Riverside is about 35 to 40 miles inland from the Hilton Anaheim: 45 minutes to an hour by car off-peak, mostly on SR-91.

The Convention is held at the Hilton Anaheim, July 19–25, 2027. Riverside is a day trip for before or after the week, not a place to stay during it. Go early in the day: inland summers are hot.

The host parish, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, is at 4700 Canyon Crest Dr, Riverside, CA 92507. Sunday Orthros is at 9:00 AM and Divine Liturgy at 10:00 AM.

EIGHT STOPS

Saint Andrew, and seven places to see

1. Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church

The host parish began in a rented room in 1989 and has been led by the Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham since 1998. Its Byzantine-style temple, modeled on the Church of St. Catherine in Thessaloniki, was consecrated on December 3, 2011. Visitors are welcome at services: Orthros on Sunday at 9:00 AM and the Divine Liturgy at 10:00 AM. Check saintandrew.net before you go, or meet the host parish.

2. The Mission Inn Hotel & Spa

Riverside’s landmark began in 1876 as a 12-room adobe guest house and grew into a sprawling Mission Revival hotel. It was named a National Historic Landmark in 1977. Walk the courtyards, or stop for a meal.

3. Mount Rubidoux

The Serra Cross on the summit was dedicated in 1907, and the Easter sunrise service held there in 1909 is often called the first of its kind in the country. About three and a half miles of trails climb to views across the city. Go at sunrise in July.

4. California Citrus State Historic Park

More than 250 acres of working groves tell the story of the citrus industry that built Riverside and Orange County alike. The visitor center is open Friday to Sunday.

5. The Parent Navel Orange Tree

At Magnolia and Arlington Avenues stands the tree grown from the navel orange stock sent to Eliza Tibbets in 1873, the start of California’s navel orange industry. It has been a state historical landmark since 1932.

6. The Cheech and the Riverside Art Museum

The Cheech Marin Center for Chicano Art & Culture opened in 2022 in Riverside’s former public library. It is run with the Riverside Art Museum a block away, and one membership covers both.

7. Castle Park, for the kids

Mini golf, rides and an arcade in a park that has been a Riverside family favorite since 1976.

8. The Fox Performing Arts Center

A 1929 Spanish Colonial Revival theater, restored and reopened in 2010, a short walk from the Mission Inn. Check the calendar for a show.

Hours and service times change; confirm before you drive. The Convention itself is in Anaheim: book inside the Convention block.
