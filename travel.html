GETTING TO THE HILTON ANAHEIM

Airports, driving routes, trains and parking for the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim, 777 W Convention Way, Anaheim, CA 92802.

Updated November 2026

ONE THING FIRST

The Convention is in Anaheim, not Riverside

The host parish, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, is in Riverside. The Convention itself is at the Hilton Anaheim.

Riverside is about 35 to 40 miles from the hotel by road: 45 minutes to an hour off-peak, and longer at rush hour. Hotels near the parish are not near the Convention, so book your room in Anaheim, inside the Convention block.

GETTING HERE

Eight ways in, and where to park

1. Fly into John Wayne (SNA)

John Wayne Airport / Orange County is the closest, about 14 miles from the hotel. It is usually the easiest arrival, especially from the West and the Mountain states.

2. Or Long Beach (LGB)

Long Beach Airport is about 18 to 20 miles away and worth checking for fares.

3. LAX and Ontario (ONT) work too

Los Angeles International (LAX) and Ontario International (ONT) are each about 35 miles from the hotel. Allow extra time from LAX in afternoon traffic. Ontario is the closest airport to Riverside.

4. Driving from Los Angeles

Take I-5 South and exit at Harbor Boulevard, then follow signs to Convention Way. It is about 26 miles from downtown Los Angeles, roughly 35 minutes without traffic.

5. Driving from San Diego

Take I-5 North and exit at Katella Avenue, turn left on Harbor Boulevard, then right on Convention Way. It is about 95 miles, close to two hours.

6. Driving from Riverside and the Inland Empire

Take SR-91 West to SR-57 South, exit at Katella Avenue and head west to Harbor Boulevard. SR-91 is one of the busiest corridors in the region, so plan around rush hour.

7. By train

Amtrak’s Pacific Surfliner and Metrolink’s Orange County Line stop at ARTIC, Anaheim’s regional transit center, about three miles from the hotel. Metrolink runs mostly on weekdays; check the timetable before you plan a weekend arrival. A taxi or rideshare covers the last few miles.

8. Parking at the Hilton Anaheim

Self-parking and valet are available in the covered garage behind the hotel, with a maximum vehicle height of 6′8″. Daily rates are set by the hotel and change, so confirm the current rate when you book.

Once you’re here, nearly everything happens under one roof. For food, beaches and family time nearby, see the Anaheim guide.
