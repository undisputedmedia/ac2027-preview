By Air

Six airports, one hotel

John Wayne Airport in Santa Ana is the closest and the easiest — a small, quick airport about twenty minutes from the hotel. Los Angeles International is the one with the most destinations. Ontario is the quiet alternative and often the cheapest.

By Train

Straight into Anaheim

Anaheim has its own Amtrak station. Amtrak Anaheim Station (ANA) sits inside the ARTIC terminal at 2626 E Katella Avenue — 3.1 miles from the hotel, about seven minutes by car.

Amtrak Pacific Surfliner
13 trains a day each way between Los Angeles and San Diego stop at Anaheim. Trains running only north of Los Angeles do not reach it.
From further afield
Four Amtrak long-distance routes end at Los Angeles Union Station — the Coast Starlight, Southwest Chief, Sunset Limited and Texas Eagle. Union Station to Anaheim is another 39 minutes on the Surfliner.
Metrolink Orange County Line
Uses the same station, about 45 minutes from Union Station. Weekend service is much thinner than weekday — the Surfliner is the safer choice on a Saturday.
Station to the hotel
3.1 miles, so not a walk. OCTA Route 50 serves the Convention Center, and rideshare and taxis wait at the front. The station takes no checked baggage and its ticket window is currently closed — Fullerton is the nearest staffed one.

Service levels are current as of September 2026. Confirm schedules at amtrak.com when you book.
