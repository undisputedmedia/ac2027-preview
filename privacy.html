PRIVACY POLICY

AC2027 SOCAL

How ac2027socal.com handles your information, for the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim.

Last updated November 5, 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

We don’t sell your information

We collect what we need to run the Convention and keep you updated, we use standard analytics to improve the site, and we never sell your personal information.

QUESTIONS

Email info@ac2027socal.com

Who we are

This site is published by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, Riverside, California, host of the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim. For any privacy question, write to info@ac2027socal.com.

Information we collect

This site collects a small amount of information:

Get Notified. If you sign up for updates, we store your email address and, if you choose to give them, your first name and parish.

Messages you send us. If you email us about sponsorship, press or anything else, we keep that correspondence so we can reply.

Usage information. Like most websites, this site records which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are (city or region), the type of device and browser, and how people arrived. See Cookies and analytics below.

We don’t run accounts, and no payment information is collected on this site. Hotel reservations, and event registration once it opens, are handled by third-party providers described below. We never see your card details.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics 4 loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how the site is used, and the Meta Pixel, to measure visits that come from our Facebook and Instagram posts and ads. These tools receive the usage information described above. You can opt out of Google Analytics in any browser with Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

The site is hosted on Wix, which sets cookies it needs to run. We may add or change these tools over time and will update this page when we do.

How we use information

We use this information to run the Convention, keep you updated if you asked us to, answer your messages, and improve this site. We don’t sell your personal information.

Who handles your information

We use a small number of service providers, and each handles information only on our behalf:

Wix.com hosts this site and stores Get Notified sign-ups and form messages.

Google provides Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager. Meta provides the Meta Pixel.

Get Notified sign-ups are shared with the Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese of North America, which sends Convention email through Mailchimp.

We don’t sell your personal information.

Third-party links and services

Several links on this site take you to services we don’t control. Each has its own privacy policy:

Hotel booking. The Reserve Your Room links go to Passkey, which runs the room block for the Hilton Anaheim.

Sponsorship, journal and volunteer forms. These go to Saint Andrew’s parish management system.

Social media. Our Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X and TikTok links go to those platforms.

Travel and attractions. Pages on this site mention airlines, Amtrak and nearby places. None of them are affiliated with the Convention.

Data retention

We keep the email addresses and messages described above only as long as needed to communicate about the Convention. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

California residents: we don’t sell your personal information. You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@ac2027socal.com.

Visitors in the European Union or United Kingdom: our basis for processing is your consent (for the mailing list) or our legitimate interest in replying to messages you send us. You can access, correct or delete your information, and withdraw consent at any time, by emailing the same address.

Children’s privacy

The Convention is a family event, but this site is meant for adults planning to attend. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, email info@ac2027socal.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update the date at the top of this page whenever this policy changes.
