Privacy Policy

Draft for design review. This policy has not been reviewed by counsel and describes the site as it exists in this preview. It should be reviewed against the Committee's actual data practices before ac2027socal.com goes live, and updated any time those practices change.

Last updated September 20, 2026.

Who We Are

This site is published by the Convention Committee for the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, Riverside, California. For any privacy question, contact info@ac2027socal.com.

Information We Collect

This site collects very little on its own:

  • Email updates. If you submit your email address through the "Get Notified" form, we store it to send you Convention updates.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us — a sponsorship inquiry, a press inquiry, a general question — we keep that correspondence to respond to it.

We do not operate accounts, and no payment information is collected on this site. Hotel reservations and, later, event registration are handled entirely by third-party providers described below — we never see your card details.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This site does not currently use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels of any kind. If that changes before launch, this section will be updated first, and a consent notice will be added if required by law.

How We Use Information

We use the information above only to run the Convention: sending the updates you signed up for, and responding to messages you send us. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share your email address with sponsors, vendors or other third parties for their own marketing.

Third-Party Links & Services

Several links on this site take you to services we don't control, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Hotel booking — the "Reserve Your Room" links go to Passkey, operating the room block on behalf of the Hilton Anaheim.
  • Social media — our Instagram, Facebook and YouTube links take you to those platforms.
  • Travel and attractions — pages on this site reference airlines, Amtrak, and nearby attractions including Disneyland Resort, none of which are affiliated with the Convention.

Data Retention

We keep the email addresses and messages described above only as long as needed to communicate about the Convention, and expect to delete or anonymize mailing list data after the 2027 Convention concludes. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Your Rights

California residents

We do not sell or share personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, so there is nothing to opt out of. You may still request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@ac2027socal.com.

EU / UK visitors

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, our basis for processing the information above is your consent (for the mailing list) or our legitimate interest in responding to messages you send us. You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your information, and to withdraw consent at any time, by emailing the address above. The Committee has not designated a separate EU representative at this time.

Children's Privacy

The Convention is a family event, but this site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The "Get Notified" list and all correspondence are intended for adults planning attendance.

Changes to This Policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page whenever this policy changes, and post material changes here before they take effect.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information can go to info@ac2027socal.com.
