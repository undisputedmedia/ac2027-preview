HOW IT WORKS

How registration works

Every Archdiocese Convention registers through the Antiochian Events Registration System, run by the Archdiocese’s Department of Convention and Conference Planning. When registration opens, the link will be here — start from this page so you land in the right place.

Everyone attending the week registers: delegates, families and visitors alike. You don’t need to be Antiochian, and you don’t need to be a delegate.

Parish delegates and groups

Parishes send delegates to the General Assembly, and delegate registration is parish-based — your parish office is the right first stop. Group organizers can email info@ac2027socal.com for the group instructions as soon as they are released.

Your hotel room is separate

Booking a room at the Hilton Anaheim doesn’t register you for the Convention, and registering won’t book you a room. The room block is open now at $209 a night. Reserve your room.
