ABOUT THE CONVENTION

ONE BODY, ONE WEEK

The 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention brings the Archdiocese together as one body for Divine Services, the General Assembly, its departments and its young people, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim.

THE GATHERING

One week, the whole Archdiocese

Clergy, delegates, families and visitors from across North America meet every two years in a different city. The 2027 Convention is hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church, Riverside, California, with the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan SABA. More answers are in the FAQ.

HOW OFTEN

Every two years, in a different host city. 2025 was Chicago; 2027 is Southern California.

WHO ATTENDS

Hierarchs, clergy and parish delegates from across North America, with their families and anyone who wants to come.

GENERAL ASSEMBLY

The Archdiocese’s business meeting, where parish delegates are seated. It’s one part of the week.

OPEN TO ALL

No need to be Antiochian or a delegate. Anyone curious about Orthodoxy is welcome.

FAMILIES

Teen SOYO and Kidz Club (ages 4–12) run all week in their own space at the hotel.

REGISTRATION

Not open yet. The room block is open now; stay up-to-date on when registration opens.

THE HOST PARISH

Saint Andrew, Riverside

Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church in Riverside is hosting the 2027 Convention. The Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham is the pastor, and the clergy and parishioners of Saint Andrew are organizing the week.

Riverside is about 35 miles inland. The Convention itself is in Anaheim, so book inside the Convention block to stay close to every session. More about Saint Andrew.
