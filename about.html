ABOUT THE CONVENTION

ONE BODY, ONE WEEK

The 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention brings the Archdiocese together as one body — for Divine Services, the General Assembly, its departments and its young people — July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim.

THE GATHERING

One week, the whole Archdiocese

Clergy, delegates, families and visitors from parishes across North America meet face to face — every two years, in a different host city.

The 2027 Convention is hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church of Riverside, California, with the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan SABA. It is the first Archdiocese Convention held in California since 2009.

Everyone is welcome. You don’t need to be a delegate to attend, and you don’t need to be Antiochian.

Every service, session and banquet takes place inside the Hilton Anaheim. Nothing is off-site.

COMMON QUESTIONS

The short answers

How often is the Convention held?

Every two years, in a different host city. The 2025 Convention was in Chicago; 2027 is Southern California’s turn.

Who attends?

Hierarchs, clergy and lay delegates from parishes across North America, with their families and anyone else who wants to come.

What is the General Assembly?

The business meeting of the Archdiocese, where parish delegates are seated and the work of the Archdiocese is discussed and decided. It is one part of the week, not the whole of it.

Do I have to be Antiochian?

No. Orthodox Christians from every jurisdiction, and anyone curious about the Orthodox faith, are welcome.

Is it a family event?

Yes. Teen SOYO and Kidz Club (ages 4–12) run all week alongside the adult program, in their own space inside the hotel.

Is registration open?

Not yet. The hotel room block is open now, and registration opens later in 2026. Read how registration works, or get notified the day it opens.
