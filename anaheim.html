ANAHEIM GUIDE

BEYOND THE CONVENTION CENTER

Where to eat, where to take the kids and where to find the ocean during the 2027 Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25 at the Hilton Anaheim.

Updated November 2026

WHY ANAHEIM

A city that feels like home

Anaheim sits in the heart of Orange County, with the Pacific about 20 minutes away and one of America’s best-known Arab-American neighborhoods ten minutes west of the hotel.

The Convention keeps you busy, but the week has evenings, and many families arrive early or stay on. The Convention room block opens on Saturday, July 17, at the same $209 rate. Here is where to spend the time.

TEN PLACES

Where to go in Anaheim and beyond

1. Little Arabia, ten minutes west

Along Brookhurst Street between Ball Road and Broadway, Arab-American families have built a neighborhood of bakeries, grocers and restaurants since the 1980s. The Anaheim City Council formally recognized Little Arabia in 2022. For many Convention families it will taste like home.

2. Kareem’s, for falafel and fatteh

Opened in 1996 by a family from Nazareth, Kareem’s (1208 S Brookhurst St) is described as the oldest Arab restaurant still operating in Little Arabia. Come hungry.

3. Manaeesh and sweets on Brookhurst

Forn Al Hara (512 S Brookhurst St) bakes Lebanese manaeesh to order. For dessert, Le Mirage Pastry (100 S Brookhurst St) makes Syrian sweets, and Al Amir Bakery (2281 W Ball Rd) is a neighborhood standby. Hours change, so check before you go.

4. Anaheim GardenWalk, a short walk

The open-air dining and shopping center a few minutes’ walk from the hotel is the easy answer for a group dinner between sessions.

5. The Anaheim Packing House

A 1919 citrus packing house turned food hall, about three miles north in downtown Anaheim. Food stalls under one roof make it ideal for a family that can’t agree on dinner. Center Street’s shops are next door.

6. A ballgame or a concert

Angel Stadium and Honda Center are both about three miles from the hotel. July is baseball season, so check the schedule for home games during Convention week.

7. The beach

Huntington Beach is about 17 miles away, roughly 20 minutes. Seal Beach is about 20 miles. Newport Beach and Laguna Beach are a little farther and worth the drive for an evening at the water.

8. Crystal Cove State Park

On the coast at Newport Coast, Crystal Cove has tide pools, bluff trails and a historic beach cottage district. About 25 miles from the hotel.

9. Museums for curious kids

In nearby Santa Ana, Discovery Cube Orange County is a hands-on science museum, and the Bowers Museum (open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM) has art and cultures from around the world. Both are about five miles from the hotel.

10. Sunday Liturgy before the Convention

Arriving early? July 18, the Sunday before the Convention, you can worship at a nearby parish: St. Luke Antiochian Orthodox Church in Garden Grove (about 5 miles), St. Barnabas Antiochian Orthodox Church in Costa Mesa (about 13 miles), or St. John the Baptist Greek Orthodox Church here in Anaheim (about 4 miles). Check each parish’s website for service times.
