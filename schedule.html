The full schedule for the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim, is coming soon. Each day is published here as the committee confirms it.

THE SHAPE OF THE WEEK

What we know so far

Every service, session, exhibit and banquet takes place inside the Hilton Anaheim. Nothing is off-site.

Every day

Divine Services with the hierarchs and clergy of the Archdiocese, workshops, department meetings, choir rehearsals, youth programming (Teen SOYO, and Kidz Club for ages 4–12), the vendor hall, and “Ask Abouna” late each night.

Once during the week

The General Assembly, Women’s Day, the Bible Bowl, the Oratorical Festival, shrine visitation with the Miraculous Kursk Root Icon, the Order of Saint Ignatius reception, the Grand Banquet, and the Hierarchical Divine Liturgy that closes the week.

Before you plan

Attendance is open: you don’t need to be a delegate or Antiochian. Some events, such as the Grand Banquet, are added on when you register. How registration works.
