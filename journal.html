LEGACY JOURNAL

ADVERTISE · HONOR · REMEMBER

The Legacy Journal is the commemorative book of the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention, July 19–25, 2027 at the Hilton Anaheim. Every attendee takes one home, and copies are kept in parishes and homes across North America.

SEE IT IN THE BOOK

Pick a placement, see it in the journal

Tap a placement to see it on a 7.5″ × 10″ page, drawn to scale, with its price.

WHAT GOES INSIDE

A page for every story

Families, parishes and businesses all have a place in the journal.

Families

Honor a loved one, remember someone who has reposed, or mark a milestone: a graduation, a wedding, an anniversary. A name listing needs no artwork at all.

Parishes and departments

Send greetings to everyone gathered in Anaheim. Churches and Archdiocese departments have their own reduced full-page rates.

Businesses

Put your name in front of Orthodox Christian families from across North America, in a book they take home and keep.

How to reserve

1 · Choose a placement from the rate card.

2 · Reserve it through the Saint Andrew reservation form.

3 · Your confirmation brings the artwork specifications and the submission deadline.

4 · Send high-resolution, print-ready artwork. Name listings need none.
