THE INVITATION

Come and See

With the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan SABA, and with joy and anticipation, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church in Riverside, California invites the entire Antiochian Archdiocese to join us for the 2027 Biennial Convention, a week of worship and fellowship in Southern California.

Set against the palm-lined promenade of Anaheim’s Resort District, steps from Disneyland and a short drive from the Orange County beaches, this Convention will bring together clergy and laity from across North America for Divine Services, edifying workshops, youth programs, department meetings, vendor exhibits and grand banquets.

The Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham, together with the clergy and parishioners of Saint Andrew, look forward to welcoming you.

Sunshine, coastline, community

Anaheim in July: steps from the Resort District, about 20 minutes from the beach, and an easy trip from four airports.
