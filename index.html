The Invitation

Come and See

With the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan SABA, and with joy and anticipation, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church in Riverside, California invites the entire Antiochian Archdiocese to join us for the 2027 Biennial Convention — a week of worship, fellowship, and celebration in the heart of Southern California.

Set against the palm-lined promenade of Anaheim's Resort District — steps away from Disneyland and a short drive to the beaches of Orange County — this Convention will bring together clergy and laity from across North America for Divine Services, edifying workshops, youth programs, department meetings, vendor exhibits and grand banquets.

The Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham, together with the clergy and parishioners of Saint Andrew, look forward to welcoming you.

Sunshine, coastline, community

Anaheim in July — walking distance from the Disneyland Resort, forty minutes from the coast, and an easy trip from four airports.

What is the Convention?

The Biennial Convention is where the Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese of North America meets as one body — for Divine Services, for the General Assembly, for its departments and its young people, and for the ordinary work of seeing one another face to face.

The 58th Biennial is hosted by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church of Riverside, California, and meets at the Hilton Anaheim from July 19 to 25, 2027. Clergy, delegates, families and visitors from parishes across the United States and Canada are expected.

Everyone is welcome. You do not need to be a delegate to attend, and you do not need to be Antiochian.

Common Questions

The short answers

How often does it happen?

Every two years. The Convention moves between host cities, and a different parish or diocese hosts each time. 2027 is Southern California's turn.

Who attends?

Hierarchs, clergy and lay delegates from parishes across North America, along with their families and anyone else who wants to come. Parishes send delegates to the General Assembly, but attendance itself is open.

What is the General Assembly?

The business meeting of the Archdiocese, where parish delegates are seated and the work of the Archdiocese is discussed and decided. It is one part of the week rather than the whole of it.

Do I have to be Antiochian?

No. Visitors are welcome, and many attendees come from other Orthodox jurisdictions or are simply curious.

Is it a family event?

Yes. Youth and children's programming runs in parallel with the adult schedule all week, and the venue sits in the middle of Anaheim's Resort District.

Is registration open?

Not yet. The hotel room block is open now; event registration opens later this year and will be announced here and by email.

The Host Parish

Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church

Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church in Riverside, California is hosting the 58th Biennial. The Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham serves as Pastor, and the clergy and parishioners of Saint Andrew are the ones organising the week.

Riverside is roughly forty miles inland from Anaheim. The Convention itself is in Anaheim — the host parish is credited here, not used as the venue.

The Shape of the Week

What runs daily, and what happens once

Every service, session, exhibit and banquet takes place inside the Hilton Anaheim. Nothing is off-site.

Every Day

  • Divine Services — Orthros, Vespers, Compline, Bible study and confessions with the hierarchs and clergy of the Archdiocese
  • Workshops — sessions across the week, several days carrying their own theme
  • Department meetings — the Archdiocese's departments convene through the week
  • Choir rehearsals — including Byzantine choir
  • Youth programming — Teen SOYO, and Kidz Club for ages 4–12
  • Vendor hall — Orthodox booksellers, iconographers and exhibitors
  • "Ask Abouna" — late each night

Once During the Week

  • The General Assembly — parish delegates seated; the business of the Archdiocese
  • Women's Day
  • The Bible Bowl
  • The Oratorical Festival
  • Shrine visitation — the Miraculous Kursk Root Icon
  • Order of Saint Ignatius reception
  • The Grand Banquet
  • Hierarchical Divine Liturgy — closing the week

Before You Plan

Three things worth knowing

Attendance is open. You do not need to be a delegate, and you do not need to be Antiochian. Parishes send delegates to the General Assembly, but the week itself is open.

Children have their own schedule. Teen SOYO and Kidz Club run in parallel with the adult program all week, in their own space in the hotel.

Some events are ticketed separately. The Grand Banquet and certain receptions are additions at registration rather than part of the base. The 2027 structure has not been published yet.
