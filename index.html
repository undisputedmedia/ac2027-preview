THE INVITATION

Come and See

With the blessing of His Eminence Metropolitan SABA, and with joy and anticipation, Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church in Riverside, California invites the entire Antiochian Archdiocese to join us for the 2027 Biennial Convention — a week of worship, fellowship, and celebration in the heart of Southern California.

Set against the palm-lined promenade of Anaheim’s Resort District — steps away from Disneyland and a short drive to the beaches of Orange County — this Convention will bring together clergy and laity from across North America for Divine Services, edifying workshops, youth programs, department meetings, vendor exhibits and grand banquets.

The Very Reverend Father Josiah Trenham, together with the clergy and parishioners of Saint Andrew, look forward to welcoming you.

Sunshine, coastline, community

Anaheim in July — walking distance from the Disneyland Resort, twenty minutes from the coast, and an easy trip from four airports.

THE WEEK

What happens over seven days

Every service, session and banquet takes place inside the Hilton Anaheim. Nothing is off-site.

Divine Services

Daily services throughout the week, culminating in the Hierarchical Divine Liturgy.

General Assembly

The Archdiocese meets in assembly. Parish delegates are seated; registration is parish-based.

Workshops & Departments

Sessions run by the Archdiocese’s departments across the week, open to all attendees.

Youth Programs

Programming for teens and children runs in parallel, with its own schedule and space.

Bible Bowl & Oratorical

The Bible Bowl and the Oratorical Festival — two of the week’s long-standing traditions.

The Grand Banquet

The closing banquet. Ticketed separately as part of registration when it opens.
