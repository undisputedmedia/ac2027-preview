Media & Press

Reporting on the 58th Biennial Antiochian Archdiocese Convention? This page has the essentials — quick facts, press coverage as it runs, brand assets, and how to reach the Convention Committee.

The Convention is the every-other-year national gathering of the Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese of North America, hosted in 2027 by Saint Andrew Orthodox Christian Church of Riverside, California, meeting at the Hilton Anaheim.
